# Noren Constitution

Noren is an open-source web app that lets people step into the stories behind real places: public posts, interviews with owners and contributor notes, shown with their sources. Nagasaki is the first region. The west coast of Peninsular Malaysia, from Kuala Lumpur to Alor Setar, is the second.

## Core Principles

### I. Stories, Not Scores (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
The product MUST NOT show or compute ratings, stars, scores, or sentiment-based labels or ordering, for any place, person or post. Practical cautions and practical facts (for example closed days, cash only, steps at the door, high chairs, a halal sign shown) are allowed only from a fixed vocabulary agreed per region. A practical fact says what is there, not whether it is good, and MUST NOT be counted, averaged or used to order places. Reviews and ratings are left to the services that already do them. A person's own collection progress, for example 2 of 6 panels, MAY be shown to that person alone. It MUST NOT be shown to anyone else, compared between people, ranked or turned into points. A plan made for several travellers MAY say which traveller a stop was chosen for. It MUST NOT count, score or rank the travellers or their turns.

### II. Real Data Only, Labelled Until Checked (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
Every place, post, person and claim shown as real MUST be real. Posts, people and claims MUST be confirmed by a human before they are shown; a member who links a post, writes a story or reports what they found at a place is that human, and it is shown with their name. The exceptions are a found public post and a story written from found posts, which are shown under the limits of Principles IV and V. A place listed from open data (for example OpenStreetMap) MAY be shown before anyone has checked it, but only with a visible "Not yet confirmed" label and a way to see where it came from and under which licence. A person who has been there confirms it ("Still here"), which replaces the label and records who and when; a signed-in member who has been to a place missing from the map MAY add it, and it counts as confirmed in person by them; a report that something is wrong goes to a person, and a place a person agrees is gone is hidden. An import MUST NOT overwrite what a person confirmed, and confirmations MUST NOT be counted into a score or ranking (Principle I). Anything created for a demo, test or video MUST belong to an explicitly labelled mock user, carry a visible "Demo" marker, and be excluded from every statistic and every export of real data.

### III. Humans Are the Source of Truth
A person-placed pin is the authoritative location of a place. A person-corrected transcript is the authoritative record of an interview. An owner-confirmed claim is the only kind of claim about a business that the product publishes as fact. What other people say about a place is published as their statement, never as the product's. What a member found at a place, reported from the region's fixed vocabulary (Principle I), is shown with their name and the date, and they MAY remove it. What a visitor wrote in a public post is shown as their own words with the source (Principle IV). The product MAY check a traveller's rule against an owner-confirmed claim, a member's report or a visitor's quoted words, and MUST say which of them the check rests on. A missing record means not known, never yes or no. When records disagree, or a report or post is older than the limit set for the region, the rule MUST be treated as not met or not checked. Openly licensed text about a place that has no business owner on record, such as a landmark, may be published only after a steward approves it, with its licence and attribution shown. Data from outside providers, including a note in an open listing, is a suggestion with a recorded source, never the truth, and MUST NOT count as a check.

### IV. Link Out, Don't Copy
Public posts MUST be shown through the originating platform's own embed, with the author credit beside it and a link to the original. For a post a member links, the product stores only the URL, platform, author link, date and a human-written note, and hides the entry when its original disappears. It MUST NOT store, summarise or run AI over the content of third-party posts, with one exception (owner, 2026-11-04). The product MAY find public posts about a place and show a short quote from one, word for word, with the author as the platform shows them, a label saying it was found in a public post, and a link to the original. For this it MAY store the post's words, run a check over them and show a machine translation (Principle VI). A found post is shown only when the check finds a story or a practical tip about that place, the post names the place and carries no advert label, and the quote holds no contact details and nothing about a person's health or death. Neither the place's owner nor the post's author is asked first. A steward MAY hide a found post, its author MAY ask for its removal, and removal MUST erase the stored words. A quote from a found post MAY back a check of a traveller's rule (Principle III) only when the quoted words themselves say it.

### V. AI Is an Editor, on Permitted Text Only
AI MAY work only on text the product is permitted to process: interview transcripts with a consent record, notes a contributor typed, openly licensed text with attribution, a story a member wrote when its writer has ticked that AI may read it, and the words of a found public post under Principle IV. Every sentence AI helps write MUST carry a footnote to the claim it came from. A sentence without a source MUST NOT be published, and a failed check MUST fail closed to human review. AI-assisted text MUST be labelled as such. A story agent MAY write a place's story from the found posts about it (owner, 2026-11-04), labelled as written by AI. Every sentence of it MUST cite words found in those posts, a second check MUST read it against those words, a sentence MUST leave the page with any post it cites, and a steward MAY withdraw the story. It never speaks for an owner.

### VI. Original Language First
The language the person spoke or wrote is canonical. Translations MUST be labelled as machine translation until a human confirms them. Place names are kept in every script people use (for example kanji, kana, romaji and English in Nagasaki, and Malay, English, Chinese and Tamil in Malaysia).

### VII. Region Is Configuration, and the Project Stays Open
Region-specific behaviour (languages, map area, enabled platforms, the vocabulary of cautions and practical facts, consent text, minimum age, retention, takedown contact, the calendar of holidays, how old a report may be, and who may use a feature on trial) MUST live in configuration, not in code. The product is one app with one map, and it MAY cover more than one region. Each region has its own configuration. A region MUST NOT be switched on before the privacy notice and terms cover it under the law that applies there. The repository MUST NOT contain credentials or personal data. Data under different licences MUST be kept apart and attributed.

## Privacy, Consent and Legal Constraints

- Accounts MAY open to anyone aged 16 or over once a privacy notice and terms exist in the languages the region needs (Japanese and English in Nagasaki; English in Malaysia, and Malay too where Malaysian law asks for it) and a takedown workflow is live. Real businesses stay under the draft rule until the full launch pack exists: a named operator of record, terms of service and creator licence, a privacy notice in the region's languages that names any transfer abroad, interview consent capture, an under-16 rule, a takedown workflow and owner verification. A region's public pages MAY be opened to search engines by the owner's decision once its privacy notice, terms and takedown workflow are live (Nagasaki: 2026-11-18). Private pages stay hidden from search. A public read-only demo MAY be shown before then, but only with mock content, openly licensed content, posts from creators who agreed, and what members share: a place a member adds, a story a member writes, what a member reports they found at a place and a public post a member links, each shown with the member's name by their agreement when they share it and removable by them at any time. Found public posts, and stories written from them, MAY be shown under Principles IV and V. A linked post needs no agreement from its author, because it is already public: it is shown only through its platform's embed with credit and a link back (Principle IV), disappears when its original does, and its author MAY ask for it to be removed. A story a member writes MAY carry photos the member took, shown with the member's name and with location and camera data removed. The form MUST ask members to add only photos they took and to ask people before showing their faces. A person shown in a photo, with or without an account, MAY ask for it to be removed through the takedown workflow, and a steward MUST then remove it. Removing a photo, or deleting the account that added it, MUST delete its file. Apart from people who appear in members' photos and the authors of linked or found posts, credited as their platform shows them, no other real person's data is shown.
- A member MAY describe the people travelling with them so that a plan fits them: a label of the member's choosing, an age band, practical needs and likes. The product MUST NOT ask for a traveller's legal name, date of birth, diagnosis, or the medical or religious reason behind a need. A description is private to the member and the people they share a plan with. It MUST NOT appear on a public page, in search, in an answer to an AI assistant or in an export, and MUST NOT be used for anything but that member's own trips. A child is described only by an adult travelling with them. A page shared with the party MUST NOT say which traveller a need belongs to. The privacy notice MUST say where a description is kept and what an AI service is sent.
- The notice, terms and consent cover each region under the law that applies there. Noren treats Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010, as amended by the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024, as applying to its Malaysian coverage and MUST follow it, including its Malay and English notice and choice requirements.
- For Malaysian coverage, Noren MUST assess every personal data breach and keep a breach register for at least two years. It MUST notify the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Commissioner as soon as practicable, within 72 hours for breaches that meet the reporting criteria, including sensitive-data breaches and breaches affecting more than 1,000 people. It MUST notify people who face actual or likely significant harm without unnecessary delay and within seven days of the initial notice to the Commissioner.
- For Malaysian coverage, Noren MUST document the lawful ground for each overseas transfer and keep proof of consent whenever consent is the ground. For trip data that reveals an identifiable person's health or beliefs, Noren MUST obtain and be able to show that person's explicit consent, or the consent of their parent, guardian or person with parental responsibility if they are under 18. Noren MUST handle electronic written portability requests under section 43A where technically feasible and the formats are compatible.
- No interview audio is uploaded, or streamed to an AI service, without a recorded consent covering publication, AI processing, transfer abroad and voice retention. Withdrawal MUST hide everything derived from that interview.
- The product is a publisher of the pages it composes, so its own wording about real businesses is held to the same standard as a human editor's.
- Research under `research/` is the evidence base for external claims. Items marked UNVERIFIED there stay unverified until checked. This constitution is not legal advice; legal questions go to a qualified lawyer.

## Development Workflow

- Development is spec-driven with GitHub Spec Kit: constitution, specify, clarify, plan, tasks, implement. Clarification MUST be completed before planning, and planning before building.
- Technology choices belong in the plan, never in the spec.
- Nothing built in this codebase is entered in the Sea x OpenAI Codex Hackathon (2026-12-05) as work done on the day, because its rules limit pre-built code. The trip agent (specs/005-trip-agent) is built here and is not kept back for that day (owner, 2026-11-23).
- A feature MAY be switched on first for accounts the owner names, as a private trial. It stays behind its region switch, and it MUST NOT open to other members until the notice and terms that describe it are live.

## Governance

This constitution supersedes other project practices. An amendment MUST be written down with its reason, update the version, and be reviewed against the active spec and plan. Versioning follows semantic rules: MAJOR for removing or redefining a principle, MINOR for adding or materially expanding one, PATCH for wording fixes. Every plan MUST include a check against Principles I to VII, and any deviation MUST be justified in writing.
