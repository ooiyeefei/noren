## What a lawyer should look at first

1. **Operator and APPI scope:** the named responsible person, actual address/location, Article 32 disclosures, request procedure, fees and security-measure information. “Open source” is not a legal entity or an exemption. Assess Malaysia's PDPA if the operator processes there or commercial activity brings it into scope (research A14); do not treat this Nagasaki pack as a Malaysian pack.
2. **Foreign transfers:** Article 28 route for each recipient, adequate country information, each recipient's measures, onward routing and contractual no-training/retention terms. `draft-1` is not enough by itself. Country summaries in privacy 6 are review material, not certification or a completed transfer assessment.
3. **Withdrawal, evidence and licensing:** narrow survival of record-keeping permission, text hidden rather than erased, audit text containing personal data, backup expiry, account deletion, moral rights and irrevocable CC licences. Do not make interviews or their AI-assisted paraphrases irrevocably open as a shortcut around withdrawal. Check ODbL derivative/collective database boundaries before any facts export.
4. **Publisher and platform duties:** Noren's responsibility for its own composed wording, defamation and neutral cautions, attribution, platform terms, external-transmission disclosure, sponsorship/stealth marketing, and disclosure orders under the Information Distribution Platform Act. The configured seven-day decision target is not proof of the separate statutory sender-inquiry process. No automatic seven-day sender consultation or disclosure-order playbook was found.
5. **Contract and age policy:** reasonable consumer liability, governing law/court, acceptance and change arrangements, authoritative language, age checks and any guardian procedure. The research describes future APPI changes; counsel must verify provision-by-provision commencement rather than calling all under-16 rules current law.

## `draft-1` consent comparison

The existing English and Japanese texts name OpenAI/United States, Anthropic/United States, Convex/Sydney/Australia and Clerk/United States. The privacy draft preserves these declared destinations and distinguishes what is actually configurable.

- **Missing recipient and countries:** `draft-1` omits Vercel hosting, Tokyo functions and the Singapore ingress edge recorded in research D17. It also omits Vercel AI Gateway as an optional processor. Gateway processing/log countries are not proven; do not replace that gap with a US-location guarantee.
- **Configurable recipients:** a `vendor/model` ID routes through Gateway and can name another vendor. An OpenAI-compatible endpoint is not necessarily OpenAI. Neither endpoint identity nor live model settings were inspected. The fixed OpenAI/Anthropic description is valid only if the actual configuration matches it.
- **Article 28 information:** `draft-1` gives provider/country names but no country-system explanation or verified recipient-protection measures. Privacy 6 adds country summaries and explicitly marks the missing recipient assessment OD-03. A draft notice does not cure consent already taken without the required pre-consent information.
- **Retention:** the event-based audio wording broadly matches `audioDays: null`. Actual completion includes dealing with all proposed statements across versions, including removals, and can leave unfinished recordings without a maximum age. `draft-1` does not explain this stalled-workflow case or continued storage of hidden text and evidence; privacy 7 does.
- **Withdrawal:** deletion of attached audio and hiding derived story material are consistent with the code. Do not interpret “hidden” as complete erasure. A provider deletion request or stopping an already sent AI request is not implemented by that withdrawal alone.
- **Other subjects and purposes:** creator profiles, visitor cookies/maps/embeds and removal-request data are outside the interview consent's scope. The broader privacy notice describes them separately; interview consent must not be presented as blanket agreement for all users.

The consent text and configuration were not changed.
