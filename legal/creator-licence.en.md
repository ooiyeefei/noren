# Noren creator licence

For owner, Japanese-language and legal review. The matching Japanese draft has not yet been reviewed by a Japanese speaker. This is a proposed permission agreement, not an active grant from existing contributors.

## 1. Who and what this covers

This licence is between you, the contributor, and **Noren, an open-source project run by Yee Fei**. It covers your original contributions: typed notes, link-card titles, place facts, interview recordings you have rights in, transcript corrections, translations and story wording. It does not give you rights in an interviewee's words or a platform post you do not own. The operator particulars remain subject to terms clause 1 (OD-01).

## 2. You keep your copyright

You keep your copyright. This is a non-exclusive licence: you may use your work elsewhere and give permission to others. Noren's open-source code licence does not put your story or recording under an open content licence. Your original language remains the source version.

## 3. Permission to run Noren

Once this agreement is approved and accepted, you give Noren permission, without a licence fee, to:

- store and host your contribution, and display the parts cleared for publication on Noren;
- transcribe consented recordings, translate permitted text, and edit or adapt it into short story statements and story text with source footnotes;
- use the processors described in the privacy notice only as needed for these tasks, including the disclosed foreign processing, subject to the interviewee's separate consent;
- show appropriate author credit and provenance, and keep limited records needed for takedowns, disputes and audit under the retention policy.

Machine translations and AI-assisted text must be labelled; a human-confirmed translation may be labelled as reviewed. Noren may not publish an owner's interview statements until the owner confirms them. This grant does not permit scraping third-party posts, AI processing of their content, unrestricted resale, model training, or use of your work in separate advertising. Provider no-training terms still need verification (privacy clause 6, OD-03).

## 4. Interviewees' permission

Before recording for upload or AI processing, explain what will be public, the named processors and countries, retention, and withdrawal in a language the interviewee understands. Record all four required consents: publication, AI processing, transfer abroad and voice retention, with the current text version. Only a steward may record assisted consent in the system.

Your copyright permission does not replace the interviewee's consent or their statement-by-statement approval. Do not include customers, staff, family or other third parties without their informed consent. Do not submit sensitive testimony in this MVP. Under-16 interviews must wait for the owner's age-policy decision and any required implemented safeguards (OD-07).

## 5. Your promises

You promise that you have the rights needed for the specific contribution; your text and corrections are honest; you do not add unsupported allegations or ratings; and you do not include another person's personal data without consent. Preserve source credits and licence details. For outside posts, supply a permitted link or embed rather than a copy.

Disclose payment, gifts, free services, commissions, employment, ownership and business direction clearly beside the contribution. Do not pass business-sponsored content off as an independent visitor account. Acceptance of paid contributions remains unresolved (OD-16).

## 6. Withdrawal and account deletion

You may ask to withdraw your contribution using [the takedown form](https://noren.yooi.me/takedown). A creator cannot use the owner's withdrawal control merely because they conducted the interview; a steward handles their request. The verified owner may withdraw interview consent in their workspace or through the form. The implemented owner withdrawal deletes stored audio, hides the transcript and derived claims, and withholds sentences citing them. It does not erase the hidden database records or other platforms' originals.

Proposed licence effect: on an accepted withdrawal, permission for future public display and adaptation of the withdrawn material ends. Only the limited permission needed to process the request and retain lawful, necessary evidence survives. This proposal does not claim a completed creator-removal or account-deletion workflow.

Hidden transcripts, consent records and audits presently remain stored. Limits for those records, account data and backups need approval (privacy clause 7, OD-05). ## 7. Place facts and open data

Original, non-personal facts such as a checked place name or pin are distinct from interview narratives and creative notes. OpenStreetMap-derived data stays subject to ODbL and its attribution and database conditions. CC BY-SA text keeps its own attribution and share-alike obligations. Noren must not relabel either as its own unrestricted facts.

[OWNER DECIDES: OD-12 — Choose a licence for independently contributed, non-personal place facts and any database rights the creator owns. Options: the limited Noren licence only, CC0, or CC BY 4.0. Recommendation: offer CC0 through a separate explicit opt-in for eligible original facts, explaining its lasting effect; keep interviews, creative text and personal data under the withdrawable Noren licence. Until that choice and opt-in exist, this draft makes no open release of creator-added facts.]

Personal information does not become free to reuse because a fact is public. Any release of a combined database needs a separate check of its ODbL boundary. Never apply CC0 to copied OSM or CC BY-SA material.

## 8. Attribution and moral rights

Credit should use the contributor's agreed public name or profile, never private account contact details. Permission to edit is limited to the service uses in clause 3; it is not permission to misrepresent what someone said.
