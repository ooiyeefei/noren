# Noren terms of service

For owner, Japanese-language and legal review. These draft terms do not take effect merely because they exist in this repository. The matching Japanese draft has not yet been reviewed by a Japanese speaker.

## 1. The service and operator

Noren is a free Japanese and English map and directory of local places in Nagasaki. It combines public place facts, links to posts and stories approved by the business owner. It has no ratings, stars, reviews or sentiment rankings. Noren does not charge for the present service.

The operator is **Noren, an open-source project run by Yee Fei**. Contact: [the takedown form](https://noren.yooi.me/takedown).

## 2. Accounts and age

Reading publicly available pages does not require an account. Accounts are invite-only during the pilot. Keep your sign-in secure and use only your own account. A steward grants creator and business-owner permissions after verification. An invitation alone gives no such permission.

The region's minimum age is 16. The interview form states that a person must be at least 16 to consent. The present system does not verify age or record guardian consent.

[OWNER DECIDES: OD-07 — Choose the under-16 policy and checks. Options: exclude under-16 accounts and interview subjects, or build and review a guardian-consent process. Recommendation: exclude them for this MVP; do not accept a guardian's signature through an unbuilt process.]

## 3. Acceptable use

- Provide truthful information you can support. Do not invent places, people, reviews or ratings, impersonate someone, harass anyone or make unsupported allegations about a business.
- Do not submit someone else's personal data without their consent. Avoid customer, staff and family details. The MVP does not accept sensitive testimony about health, survivor status or similar matters; pause an interview and ask a steward if these arise.
- Do not disguise advertising as an independent story. Disclose payment, gifts, free meals, commissions, employment, ownership and any business direction of the content clearly beside it. Owner stories must be recognisable as coming from the owner.
- Use another platform's post only through a permitted official embed or a link, under that platform's terms. Keep author credit and the original link. Do not scrape or upload copied captions, images or reviews, or use third-party post content as AI input.
- Do not bypass access controls, consent checks or takedowns, upload harmful material, or interfere with the service.

## 4. Places, stories and approval

Public facts about a real place can be shown after a steward checks the place. The business owner's approval is for their own story, rather than for the existence of the directory entry. Real-place publication remains subject to the launch gate.

An interview needs recorded consent for publication, AI processing, transfer abroad and voice retention before audio upload or processing. A corrected transcript is the source record. The verified owner can confirm, edit or remove each proposed statement about their business. Only confirmed statements may become the owner's published story. AI-assisted text is labelled, sentences cite their source claims, and translations remain labelled machine translation until reviewed. A citation does not guarantee accuracy.

Creators keep their copyright, subject to the separate [creator licence](creator-licence.en.md). Personal data is described in the [privacy notice](privacy.en.md). Neither document replaces interview consent.

## 5. Removal, withdrawal and appeals

Anyone may use [the takedown form](https://noren.yooi.me/takedown) without signing in. Give the page address and reasons. Contact details are optional. Save the receipt code privately: it lets its holder check the outcome and, when available, appeal.

The region policy is acknowledgement within **24 hours** and a reasoned decision within **7 days**. The form currently gives an immediate receipt; decisions require a steward. These times are an operating commitment, not an automatic decision mechanism. A privacy or minor report immediately hides a matching public place pending review. If the supplied address cannot be matched, automatic hiding is not possible; a steward must investigate.

A request with a decided outcome can be appealed through the status page using the receipt code and a reason. The present interface does not offer an appeal for a closed request; use the form again and refer to the earlier request if needed.

A verified owner can withdraw an interview's consent through their workspace, or ask through the form. Withdrawal deletes its stored recording, hides its transcript and claims from the reading interface, and withholds sentences citing those claims. Hidden records remain in the database; withdrawal is not complete erasure. Already downloaded copies and other platforms' originals are outside Noren's control. Account deletion is a separate request; there is no automatic account-deletion cascade. See privacy clauses 7 and 8.

Noren may hide harmful or infringing material during review. A directory entry based on independently sourced public facts does not automatically disappear when an interview is withdrawn. Noren remains responsible for the wording it publishes; it does not claim an intermediary exemption for its own story text.

Loading an embed or following a link connects you to another service under its own terms and privacy rules.

## 7. Demo content

Fictional places, people and stories are marked **Demo** and use mock accounts. They are examples, not claims about real businesses. Mock records are excluded from real-data statistics and exports. A fictional place does not remove the need for permission to show a real person's post.

## 8. Availability and accuracy

Noren is an early service. We cannot promise uninterrupted availability, complete coverage, current opening information or error-free transcription and translation. Check material details with the place itself. Report errors through the form. This wording does not remove duties or remedies that applicable law requires.
