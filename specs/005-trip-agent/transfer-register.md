The ground column records either the evidence for section 129(3)(a) consent or a candidate for the operator to document. **UNVERIFIED** means the ground actually relied on has yet to be confirmed. Section 129(3)(f) requires reasonable precautions and due diligence. Section 129(3)(b) requires necessity for a core contractual obligation and an assessment of feasible alternatives. Vendor terms alone do not establish either ground. Separate consent requires advance notice and a maintained record. The trip receipt stores the wording version and first acceptance time for the organiser. Independent evidence of which button was pressed, and evidence from companions and responsible adults, is **UNVERIFIED**.
